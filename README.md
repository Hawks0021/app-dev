# My Favorite series: The Apothecary Diaries

## About My Favorite Series

One of my favorite anime series is **The Apothecary Diaries**. It is about a girl named Maomao who has a lot of knowledge about medicine and poisons. She works inside the Imperial Palace, where she gets involved in different mysteries and uses her knowledge to solve them. I like this show because the story is interesting and different from other anime that I have watched. I enjoy how Maomao solves problems using her intelligence and curiosity. I also like the mystery, comedy, and the interactions between her and the handsome guy named jinshi.
